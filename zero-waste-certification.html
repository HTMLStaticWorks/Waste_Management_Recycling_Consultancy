What is TRUE Zero-Waste Certification?

Administered by GBCI (Green Business Certification Inc.), TRUE is the leading global rating system for tracking facility zero-waste achievements. To be eligible for certification, a facility must demonstrate a **diversion rate of 90% or higher** over a consecutive 12-month period, ensuring packaging, scrap, and organic materials are kept out of municipal landfills and incinerators.

EcoAudit has a dedicated team of TRUE advisors on staff who handle baseline inventorying, policy formatting, down-market contract building, and submission preparation.

GBCI Credit Category Areas

To gain credits, operations must show rigorous systems in place across these categories:

Redesign & Source Reduction

Reviewing vendor supply criteria, eliminating disposable items from offices, and opting for raw inputs with minimal initial packaging layers.

Composting & Organic Recovery

Implementing biological sorting processes, collecting kitchen remains, and aligning with industrial composting networks to avoid landfill decay.

Leadership & Upstream Strategy

Setting executive zero-waste boards, training managers, logging corporate policy templates, and verifying broker sustainability compliance.
