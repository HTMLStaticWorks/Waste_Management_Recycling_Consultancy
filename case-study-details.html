Maximizing Stream Purity & Slashing Landfill Disposal Fees

Our client, a tier-1 global automotive systems supplier, operated a 340,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility facing steep municipal solid waste surcharges. Despite standard recycling boxes, heavy contamination resulted in entire cardboard and plastic bins being routed to local landfill sites, costing over $15,000 monthly in tipping fees.

96.4% Diversion Rate
1,840 t Waste Diverted / Yr
$180k Saved Annually

1. The Challenge

Mixed manufacturing components—composed of composite cardboard boxes, stretch wraps, plastics, and minor metal scraps—accumulated rapidly in assembly zones. Unclear sorting indicators and high worker turnaround times resulted in a contamination rate exceeding 45%, rendering commodity streams unsellable to brokers.

2. Our Strategy

EcoAudit deployed three industrial engineers to map material flow steps from assembly to dock:

  • Container Segregation: Standardized multi-lingual container systems matching USGBC TRUE color coding color schemes.
  • On-Site Processing: Integrated two vertical cardboard balers at key shipping ports, compressing fiber volumes by 80%.
  • Workforce Training: Hosted dedicated shift seminars logging 95% worker attendance, combined with clear photo-rich labels.
  • Broker Sourcing: Re-negotiated brokerage collection contracts, guaranteeing rebates for plastic wraps.

3. Final Outcomes

Within 9 months of program implementation, stream contamination dropped below 4%. The facility successfully diverted over 1,800 metric tons of materials, completely eliminating tipping fee surcharges and securing official TRUE Gold rating status.

Project Metadata

  • Client: Global Auto Systems Corp
  • Facility Size: 340,000 sq. ft.
  • Primary Streams: LDPE wrap, Corrugated cardboard, Metal remnants
  • Certification: TRUE Gold Certified
  • Timeline: 9 Months Implementation
