Closed-Loop Material Engineering & Packaging Redesign

Our Circular Supply Chain program targets packaging overhead and production scrap. We analyze intake materials, trace logistics routes, and audit downstream handlers. By eliminating single-use wooden pallets, custom plastic trays, and heavy shrink wraps, we insulate clients from rising logistics costs and carbon taxation penalties.

Intake Reduction

Redesigning supplier packaging standards to enforce reusable crates, eliminating inbound box sorting waste entirely.

Outflow Conversion

Converting manufacturing off-cuts and trimming remnants into premium raw inputs for adjacent local industries.

Our Three-Phase Roadmap

We execute circular supply transitions systematically to protect factory runtimes:

01

Material Flow Analysis (MFA)

We log all inbound packaging types, weighing cardboard, wood, stretch film, and straps to calculate exact packaging-to-product ratios.

02

Alternative Material Verification

Our engineers source alternative compostable materials and test structural capacities inside environmental chambers.

03

Downstream Vendor Brokerage

We secure secondary material purchasing agreements with industrial recyclers, ensuring scrap offsets client tipping overhead.
