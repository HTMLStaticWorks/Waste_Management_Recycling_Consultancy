FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions regarding environmental advisory, carbon emission tracking, and regional waste policies.

What is Scope 3 waste emission tracking?

Scope 3 waste emissions cover indirect emissions generated by third-party waste hauling, processing, and downstream recycling services, including methane released in landfills from discarded office or factory solids.

How does your Zero-Waste certification program work?

We audit physical material outputs, restructure sorting schemes, train on-site logistics teams, and compile verified TRUE Certification data streams to substantiate diversion scorecards.

What are landfill tipping fees and landfill surcharges?

Tipping fees are waste disposal costs charged by weight at disposal hubs. Surcharges are punitive environmental taxes added for organic or toxic materials sorted incorrectly.

What types of businesses do you consult for?

We assist multi-tenant commercial real estate groups, manufacturing suppliers, university systems, and luxury hospitality chains needing structured waste redirection services.
